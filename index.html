Sacred Festival of Shakti & Spiritual Renewal • Class 6A Presentation

Slide 1 — Overview & Mythology

What is Navratri?

A sacred nine-night Hindu festival honoring Goddess Durga, celebrating the ultimate victory of good over evil.

Spiritual Renewal

Navratri honors Goddess Durga and marks a sacred period dedicated to intense prayer, spiritual renewal, inner reflection, and community harmony across nine divine nights.

The Durga & Mahishasura Legend

Created by the unified light of the gods, Goddess Durga was bestowed celestial weapons to defeat Mahishasura—a buffalo demon invincible to men and gods. She battled him over nine nights, vanquishing him to restore cosmic order.

Slide 2 & 3 — Significance & Traditions

Spiritual Significance & Regional Diversity

Navratri symbolizes the triumph of divine feminine energy (Shakti) over ignorance and ego.

Spiritual Core

Represents an inward spiritual journey transcending gross impurities to pure divine consciousness, culminating in the realization of the ultimate Self on Vijayadashami.

Western & Central

Gujarat: Energetic Garba and Dandiya Raas in open grounds.
Maharashtra: Devi worship alongside local traditions honoring Khandoba.

Eastern & Southern

West Bengal & Odisha: Grand Durga Puja pandals, crafts, and music.
South India: Golu displays featuring dolls arranged in tiered stories of faith.

Slide 4, 5 & 6 — Practices & Food

Fasting, Celebrations & Sattvik Cuisine

A blend of devotional discipline, vibrant dance forms, and region-specific prasad.

Devotional Rituals

Fasting with intention using fruit or sattvic diets (avoiding grains, garlic, and onions). Invoking a daily form of Durga with flowers, aarti, and Durga Saptashati recitations.

Music, Dance & Decor

Rhythmic Garba and Dandiya dance circles around decorated lamps. Spaces are beautified with marigolds, glowing diyas, string lights, and intricate colorful rangolis.

Regional Prasad & Feasts

North/West: Kuttu puri, Sabudana khichdi, Farali dhokla.
East: Royal Bhog with Khichuri & Labra.
South: Legume Sundal & Sweet Pongal.
